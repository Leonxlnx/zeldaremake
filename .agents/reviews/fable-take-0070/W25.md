It does not judge the Astra combined scene.

## Visible progress

The round side window is visible again in B. The left roof shoulder and outer rim are less uniform, the doorway is visibly wider, and the warm room details now sit in a darker recess. In the same normalized doorway rectangle used in the author's note (x 0.75–0.83, y 0.40–0.54), the original JPEGs give luma p90 0.446 in take69, 0.375 in take70, and 0.354 in the locked reference. Pixels above 0.40 fall from 19.75% to 5.54%, against 3.65% in the reference. Those are supporting image observations, not object masks or new pass thresholds; they corroborate the visible improvement in localized interior light.

Preserve the restored window clearance, wider opening, darker recess, olive moss mapping and living foliage. The author reports five new burls; at normal B viewing size they add only modest visible breakup to the large wall/support surfaces.

## Remaining W25 mismatch

The decisive remaining difference is the construction of the entrance and crown. In actual B, the entrance still reads as broad smooth ochre wall fins and slender forked supports beneath a continuous sloping moss sheet. The locked B reference has a thick, gnarled arch whose sides and projecting boughs flow into the roof edge, with stronger breaks between moss masses. The new lobes soften the outline, but the cap and supports still read as separate, relatively regular parts. Merely matching the opening's horizontal endpoints does not resolve that shape relationship.

Original owner boards03/04 reinforce the same grown-trunk construction: thick twisted branch pillars, bark ridges wrapping the arch and window boss, and moss draped over an irregular branch rim. Board08 carries that form into gameplay scale. These boards help explain the intended construction; the formal W25 judgment remains against locked shot B rather than extra criteria invented from the boards.

Suggested next structures work: make the existing jamb/root surfaces read as a continuous rounded, knotted arch and connect the visible bough forms through the eave. Retain the newly corrected opening width and window clearance while reviewing the fixed B image. A broad global light change or additional small decorations would not by itself close this form mismatch.

## A/F and D control

A and F confirm the changed crown/branch silhouette and the darker room. Their persistent house position beside the stair flight differs from the shot references; that is wider composition context, not the deciding W25 reason. Character/UI are excluded.

D is not an unchanged whole-frame control: take70 visibly moves the house bough and reveals the round window at the right edge. The left/central x 0.00–0.70 band has mean absolute RGB difference 0.019/255 from take69, while the right house rectangle x 0.75–1.00, y 0.00–0.60 has 4.994/255. This supports a localized right-house change. The published D SSIM falls 0.0056 to 0.2992; this full-take comparison cannot assign that fall specifically to cap batching. The distant huts retain their flat bright openings and simple caps/platforms from take69; their authored attachment/depth follow-up remains separate.
