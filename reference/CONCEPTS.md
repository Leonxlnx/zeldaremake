## 01_foliage_tree_materials.jpg — Foliage & tree material reference
- **Mossy tree trunk**: ancient trunks with thick bark plates, moss sheets, lichen and small plants
  growing in the bark; soft, damp, earthy. → trees: bark texture with lichen flecks + small plant
  tufts on the lower 3 m; moss creeping up the north side of the giants.
- **Branch bridge**: a fallen tree forming a natural bridge, moss-covered, vines, three hanging
  lantern pods. → the log arch (frame 56 s) carries moss, vines and pods; the fence/branch bridge on
  the plateau is the same vocabulary.
- **Roots**: exposed, gnarled, blending into rocks and paths, moss on top. → trees roots: more
  reach, root tops mossy, roots crossing path edges and wrapping boulders.
- **Hanging lanterns**: plant pods on a hooked twig; closed pod (green), open/lit pod (amber leaf
  shell with a warm core). Used near homes and paths. → lanterns: closed unlit pods among the lit
  ones; pod = leaf sepals around a glowing core.
- **Leaves & plants**: Kokiri leaf (heart-shaped), forest leaf (broad ovate), fern, ground leaf
  (round), ivy/vine. Broad, waxy, slightly stylised, painterly. → vegetation/foliage card shapes.
- **Flower clumps**: white forest flowers, purple forest flowers, unopened fern buds (fiddleheads).
  Small clusters along paths, near roots and rocks. → vegetation: add WHITE clusters alongside the
  violets; fiddleheads at fern clumps.
- **Fairy motes**: neutral white, warm yellow, soft green, in-motion trails. → atmosphere motes:
  three tints, soft trails.
- **Environment context**: a tree-house with a wide arched door, hanging pods, a stone path with
  moss edges; a stone stair with mossy risers between boulders.
