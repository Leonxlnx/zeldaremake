The loudest moment the game makes, measured as takes rather than a take.

`worstcase.mjs` records the LIVE graph (running and jumping continuously with the score playing,
seventy seconds), so unlike everything else on this lane it is not deterministic. Every figure the
lane has quoted from it was one take read to a tenth of a decibel. Below is every take taken for
this report, through `verdict.py`. True peak is 4x-oversampled, in dBFS, after the master trim.

  the bridge, mid-span over the ravine    --legs 3.8,33,180;4.0,41,0;3.8,37,90;4.0,35,270

    build     take 1   take 2   take 3     mean   range      integrated loudness (LUFS)

    after      -6.9     -6.8     -8.3     -7.33     1.5      -24.4  -24.4  -24.4

  under the lantern bough                 --legs 1.5,-4,180;1.5,-12,0;-1.5,-6,90;3.5,-8,270

    before     -8.8                       -8.80       —      -25.0
    after      -9.1    -10.1    -10.1     -9.77     1.0      -25.4  -25.4  -25.5

Three things come out of it.

1. The Q conversion did not move the worst case. The difference in means at the bridge is 0.34 dB
   against a spread of 1.5 to 1.7 dB in either condition, and the two sets of takes interleave
   (before contains both the loudest take of all, -6.7, and the quietest, -8.4). The first pair of
   takes said the peak had risen 1.0 dB; that was noise, and so would "it fell 1.0 dB" have been.

2. The instrument repeats to about +/-0.85 dB. Anything this lane has read off it to a tenth was
   read off noise.

3. The bridge really is the loudest place, and by more than was published. On the same build the
   bridge runs -6.8 to -8.3 and the bough -9.1 to -10.1: the ranges do not overlap, and the means
   are 2.4 dB apart. Adding the two takes from the other build does not close it either — the
   loudest bough take anywhere is -8.8, still under the quietest bridge take anywhere, -8.4.
   `2026-09-26-ceiling` claimed 1.1 dB from single takes and had no way to know; the conclusion
   was right and the margin was understated by half.

The loudest take of the ten is the bridge's -6.7 dBFS, which is -15.7 before the +9 dB trim.
`WORST_CASE_PEAK_DBFS` in src/audio/level.test.mjs is now that figure, where it was -16.6.

Nothing clipped in any of the ten takes, and every one is more than 3 dB under the gate.
