
where the harp's energy lands, over one 16-bar pass

  pan rule                                      quiet pass             full pass
                                          lean    L over R      lean    L over R
  as it ships: pan = the note's index    -0.116      +1.01 dB    -0.069      +0.60 dB
  …sweeping the other way each bar      +0.009      -0.08 dB    +0.028      -0.25 dB
  …sweeping the other way each phrase    -0.009      +0.08 dB    -0.009      +0.08 dB

  lean is (R−L)/(R+L): 0 is centred, negative is left.
  A pass is 16 bars; the tune alternates quiet and full passes, so both have to be near zero.

  full  pass: 87 notes kept at mean pan -0.036, 41 dropped at mean pan +0.077
         every rule that thins the figure takes from the right, so what is left leans left

  quiet pass: 50 notes kept at mean pan -0.078, 78 dropped at mean pan +0.050
         every rule that thins the figure takes from the right, so what is left leans left
