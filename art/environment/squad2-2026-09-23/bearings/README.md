# Does the middle distance populate at the other bearings?

Every "the trees do not populate" measurement this lane has made looks **north** — the owner's 06:50
screenshot, the clearing, the grove, the arch. This checks the other three bearings from the same
standing point (the plaza, `0.5, 1.6, 1.0`, a level-ish gaze), because a bearing nobody renders is a
bearing nobody has evidence for.

`band.mjs` reads the middle-distance band (rows y 0.1–0.45). The signature of haze is a high mean
with an across-column sd under about 10 (one wash, no verticals); the signature of a populated
middle distance is a lower mean with sd in the twenties (trunks and crowns interrupting).

| bearing | band mean | across-column sd | within-column sd | reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| east (the main flight) | 74.2 | 15.3 | 23.4 | trees: the flight, the treehouse door, trunks and crowns behind at 20–60 m |
| west (the deck path) | 74.2 | 22.6 | 23.2 | trees: the strongest column variation of the three |
| south (the bridge exit) | 92.5 | 19.7 | 20.4 | trees, brighter: the exit is the open end, so more air in the band |

No bearing reads as haze. The weakest column variation is east at 15.3, and that is the flight's own
masonry filling the band's centre rather than a wash — the frame shows trunks and crowns either side
of it.

Nothing to change in lane 2 from this; it closes the question of whether the north was a special case.
