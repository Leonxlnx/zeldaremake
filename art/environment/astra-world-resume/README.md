## Botanical basis and scope

Moss cushions are dense colonies of individual leafy shoots, with a living surface over
older material. Their leaves are small and usually taper toward the tip. That supports a
low, densely leafy colony with darker interstices instead of a smooth green dome.
Sources: [Australian National Botanic Gardens — What is a moss?](https://www.anbg.gov.au/bryophyte/what-is-moss.html)
and [Bryophyte leaves](https://www.anbg.gov.au/bryophyte/bryophyte-groups-leaves.html).

All geometry is original procedural work. No images, textures, models or other assets were
copied from those sources. No added dependency. The hardscape call opts into floor moss;
the shared boulder plant caller retains its existing geometry. Scatter locations, RNG streams,
instance transforms, joint tints, ground seats and the shared material are preserved.

  seed, then raycasts the frozen camera's native pixels. Texture images are stubbed only in
  this CPU identity probe; cushion material has no texture maps. Production source is unmodified.
- `capture-cushions.mjs` records current-world full and cushion-hidden images.
- `freeze-cushions.mjs [directory] [candidate-sha]` freezes candidate and baseline production

- `capture-pair.mjs [directory] [optional-label]` captures 8 fixed views from those builds and
  asserts real native GPU, exact bundle provenance, clean audit and no rendering errors.
- `compare-pair.mjs [directory]` verifies matched assets, settings, camera, lighting, browser
  and renderer, then reuses the repository's image comparison for reference metrics.
