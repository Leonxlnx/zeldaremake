# Owner distance-clarity study

The new September 22 owner direction asks for clear forest visibility, superseding the older compressed-video haze fit. The marked owner screenshot has (1) a nearly uniform cool grey middle/far forest, (2) large smooth crown forms, and (3) much stronger contrast in the near ground than the trees behind it. Owner concepts 01/07 show useful depth and shafts while retaining readable branches and leaf silhouettes. Geometry/atlas defects are the separate distance lane; haze cannot create missing leaf detail.

At parent 664f3418, `heightfog.ts` mixes bright cool air over every fogged material after applying extra distance shade. Base extinction is 0.018/m after 5 m; beyond 49 m another 0.055/m accumulates. Surface shade reaches 0.3 by 44 m. For a northward eye-level ray at 1.8 m, the retained height mist brings total veil to 83.01% at 60 m: only 5.10% of a non-emissive surface's incoming radiance survives. At 80 m the cap leaves 4.2%. These coefficients, rather than a uniform exposure problem, explain why different far albedos converge on grey.

The candidate changes only three existing defaults: base extinction 0.008/m, added far extinction 0.008/m, minimum distance shade 0.65. At 60 m the veil remains 50.62%, but 32.10% of surface radiance survives; at 80 m the veil remains 66.20% and surface contribution 21.97%. The low mist, directional air colours, horizon/sky, height/elevation profiles, fog cap, actual shadow maps, material floors, shafts and post grade are unchanged. The existing thin-near extinction 0.008/m now matches the base, so the 8–22 m catch-up bend becomes a continuous slope without shader edits. This is a look change with retained atmospheric layering, not an attempt to improve the old haze-fit score.

Post stack diagnosis: video softening is already false. The renderer uses FXAA, no TAA, and default renderScale 1; there is no active global defocus to turn off. Grade contrast 0.93/lift 0.003 and local ray intensity 0.32 can lift darks, but they do not explain the distance-selective loss and remain unchanged. Ground mist consists of low terrain-seated sheets/billboards, not the high-crown veil.
