# "ONE CRUMB." - art direction

A 10-second, 2.39:1, 24 fps short in the visual language of *Spider-Man: Into the
Spider-Verse* / *Across the Spider-Verse* (Sony Pictures Imageworks). No dialogue.
The technical bible is `docs/research/SPIDERVERSE_STYLE.md`; this file is the creative one.
Where they disagree, the research wins on technique and this file wins on taste.

## Logline

Night. A Brooklyn diner, closing time. On a teal Formica table, a small glossy-black ant with
red markings spots a heroic chunk of chocolate-chip cookie glowing under the pendant lamp. It
sprints. A diner mug slams down in its path - KRAKOOM - coffee leaps, the table jumps. The ant
skids, runs straight up the wall of the mug like a certain wall-crawler, launches off the rim,
lands, and snatches the crumb. Freeze. Comic panel. "CRUMB."

## The one rule

**Nothing is soft.** Every smooth gradient becomes a step; every step edge becomes a screen;
every out-of-focus area becomes offset colour plates; every fast motion becomes a chromatic
trail or a smear, never a blur. If a frame has a smooth gradient in it, the frame is wrong.

## World design

Scale is macro: world units are millimetres. The ant is ~7 mm long. The camera lives at ant
height (2-15 mm off the table) with very wide lenses, so ordinary diner objects read as
architecture - sugar grains are boulders, the salt shaker is a skyscraper, the mug is a
cathedral.

| Element | Design |
| --- | --- |
| **Tabletop** | Teal/cyan Formica with the classic 1950s "boomerang" and star-fleck pattern, a chrome-banded edge in the distance. Light scratches and a wiped coffee ring. It is the ground plane of every shot, so it must carry halftone beautifully. |
| **Sugar grains** | Scattered crystalline cubes 0.4-1.2 mm - boulders at ant scale, faceted, catching highlight dots. Denser near the torn sugar packet. |
| **Sugar packet** | Torn, crumpled pink paper packet with a bold printed stripe; the ant's starting ridge in shot A. |
| **The crumb** | A heroic chocolate-chip cookie chunk, ~11 mm, golden-brown with a molten chocolate chunk, cracked baked surface, sugar sparkle. Lit like treasure under a warm pendant pool. |
| **The mug** | Heavy off-white American diner mug, thick lip, red double stripe, ~95 mm tall, 82 mm diameter, foot ring. Full of black coffee. It enters from above frame (implied hand) and slams. |
| **Background monuments** | Chrome napkin dispenser, glass sugar pourer with steel cap, salt and pepper shakers, ketchup bottle, a fork lying flat, a folded check. Designed as simple graphic volumes; they recede into misregistration and halftone. |
| **Window** | Behind the table: a big diner window, night city outside. Neon signs (pink "DINER" script, cyan "OPEN", a red-orange arrow), traffic light glows, a street lamp. Rendered as flat graphic shapes with halftone glow screens and heavy misregistration - never as photographic bokeh. |
| **Pendant lamp** | Warm amber cone of light pooling on the crumb - the hero key light of the whole film. |

## Character design - the ant

- Glossy black exoskeleton with **Miles-red** accents: red stripe down the thorax, red on the
  gaster bands and the mandible tips. Reads instantly against teal.
- Appealing, not scientific: slightly oversized head, big glossy compound eyes shaped and lit
  like **Spider-Man mask lenses** - a white graphic highlight shape that squints and widens with
  the acting. Elbowed antennae with a thick scape and segmented funiculus: the ant's most
  expressive feature, animated on their own frame rate (3s, per the Hobie rule).
- Built from rigid segments (it is an exoskeleton): head, mandibles, thorax (mesosoma), petiole
  node, gaster, six legs of coxa-femur-tibia-tarsus, antennae of scape + funiculus. Rigid
  segments mean exact motion vectors and clean ink lines.
- Fine detail pass: setae (bristles) on legs and gaster, tarsal claws, tibial spurs, plate seams
  that carry crease ink lines, subtle pitting on the head.
- Shading: 4 bands, hue-shifted shadows (black -> deep indigo, not grey), a hard specular shape
  (designed highlight, not a smooth lobe), magenta/cyan rim lights from the neon.

## Shot list

**A - THE SIGHTING (0-35).** 21 mm, ultra-low, dutch -3.5. Foreground left: the crumpled pink
sugar packet ridge, huge. The ant pops up over it into silhouette against the window neon.
Across a vast teal plain, the crumb glows in its amber pool. Antennae perk (on 3s). f24: FREEZE
(6 frames) - caption box "ONE CRUMB." snaps in on 2s, a yellow radial burst around the crumb.

**B - THE SPRINT (36-79).** 28 mm side-on truck, camera on 1s moving with the ant, ant on 2s.
Legs smear into multiples. Sugar boulders whip through the near foreground as pure offset colour
plates (misregistration). Horizontal speed lines, a magenta zip ribbon trailing. "SKITT".

**C - INTO LENS (80-113).** 16 mm, ant charges straight at a lens 3 mm off the table.
Radial speed lines. f100: a hard-edged shadow sweeps over everything - palette drops to indigo.
The ant's head snaps up; eye lenses widen.

**D - THE MUG (114-137).** 18 mm, looking up from the ant. The mug's foot ring and belly fill
the frame, descending, backlit so it is a near-silhouette with a cyan Kirby-crackle rim. The
pendant lamp flares graphically behind it (halftone flare). Glitch tear f130-135.

**E - SLAM (138-157).** Contact on f138. Two graphical FLASH FRAMES (flat colour field, black
silhouettes, line screens) then a 3-panel split: (1) the foot ring hitting, sugar grains
jumping; (2) coffee leaping out of the mug in a drawn, graphic splash; (3) the ant airborne,
eyes wide. "KRAKOOM" across all three. Camera shake, channel tear.

**F - THE SWERVE (158-201).** 20 mm. The ant skids ("SKRRT"), plants, and runs up the curved
wall of the mug and around it (camera orbits with it) - a wall-crawl. f176-183: style break
into Spider-Punk xerox collage. It launches off the rim in a big graphic arc ("VWOOP").

**G - THE GRAB (202-239).** 26 mm. Landing, a sliding stop through sugar grains, the mandibles
clamp the crumb, it hoists it overhead. f228: FREEZE into a printed comic panel with a border,
caption "CRUMB.", sparkle and radial burst.

## Timing and acting

- Ant on **2s**, dropping to **1s** for the slam reaction and the wall-run (`beats.js`).
  Antennae on 3s. Mug on 2s. Camera always on 1s. 2D FX always on 2s.
- Strong key poses held 3-6 frames; fast breakdowns; anticipation before every big move;
  overshoot and settle; smear frames (stretched legs, multiples) on the fastest in-betweens.
- The camera has weight: shake with decay on the slam, whip transitions between shots, but
  motion is always a chromatic trail, never a blur.
